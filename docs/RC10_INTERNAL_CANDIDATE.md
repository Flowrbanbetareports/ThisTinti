# RC10 internal candidate

Version: `3.4.0-alpha.7-rc.10`.

RC10 is the product-quality candidate after the immutable `3.4.0-alpha.7-rc.9` Public Preview. It focuses on terminology, numerical presentation, information hierarchy, advanced-tool disclosure, activity context and frontend modularity.

It does not publish itself, replace RC9 assets, contact external parties or change the rule that every document finding requires human verification.

## Required gates

- complete repository verification and minimum 90% coverage;
- Chromium product-polish, onboarding, evidence, recovery, links and diagnostics checks;
- apparel OCR benchmark and local pilot toolkit;
- PostgreSQL and self-hosted proofs;
- Windows build, upgrade, installed diagnostics, uninstall and data-preservation lifecycle;
- immutable RC9 publication evidence preserved until a separate RC10 publication request.
