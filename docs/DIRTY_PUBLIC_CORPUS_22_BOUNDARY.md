# Dirty Public Corpus 22 — evidence boundary

This suite strengthens evidence about parser robustness on raw external files. It does not upgrade the project's pilot evidence level.

A future PASS may support statements such as:

- selected raw public purchase-order PDFs are parsed according to the frozen assertions;
- selected pinned Peppol/EN16931 documents are accepted or rejected as expected;
- the parser does not crash on this frozen corpus.

It must not be used to claim:

- universal real-world accuracy;
- a completed 30-case authorized company pilot;
- production certification;
- legal, accounting or tax correctness outside the encoded assertions;
- OCR performance on arbitrary scans.

The controlled pilot gate remains separate and unchanged.
